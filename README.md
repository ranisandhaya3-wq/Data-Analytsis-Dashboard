# Data-Analytsis-Dashboard
## Project Objective
I created an interactive Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard in Excel to analyze sales trends, customer behavior, store performance, and top-selling products using data visualization and business insights.
## Data Used




## Tools Used
 * Microsoft Excel – Data analysis and dashboard creation
 * Pivot Tables – Summarized and organized sales data
 * Pivot Charts – Visualized sales trends and performance
 * Slicers – Added interactive filtering to the dashboard
 * Conditional Formatting – Highlighted key patterns and insights
 * Data Cleaning – Prepared and structured raw data for analysis
 * KPI Metrics – Measured total sales, footfall, and average bill

## Business Question(KPIS)
1. How do sales vary by day and hour?
2. What are the peak sales periods?
3. What is the monthly sales revenue?
4. How do sales differ across store locations?
5. What is the average bill per customer?
6. Which products generate the highest sales?
7. How do sales vary across product categories?

 ## Business Questions & Answers

 1. How do sales vary by day and hour?
Ans- Sales were highest during morning hours, especially between 8 AM and 10 AM.
 2. Are there peak sales periods?
Ans- Yes, the dashboard showed peak customer activity during morning periods.
3. What is the total sales revenue?
Ans- Total sales revenue recorded was approximately $698,812.
4. How do sales vary across store locations?
Ans- Store performance varied slightly, with some locations generating higher revenue and customer footfall.
5. What is the average bill per person?
Ans- The average customer bill was approximately $4.69.
6. Which products are best-selling?
Ans- Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai Tea, and Hot Chocolate generated the highest sales.
7. How do sales vary by product category?
Ans- Coffee contributed the largest share of overall sales, followed by Tea and Bakery.

## Project Insights

* Sales activity was highest during morning hours, indicating strong customer demand during peak coffee hours.
* Coffee contributed the largest share of overall sales compared to other product categories.
* Sales performance varied across store locations, with some stores generating higher revenue and customer footfall.
* A few products consistently performed better and contributed significantly to total sales.
* Customer spending patterns showed stable average order values across the selected period.
* Interactive dashboard visualization made it easier to identify trends, compare performance, and support business decision-making.
* The project demonstrated how Excel can be used to convert raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

## Conclusion

This project helped me improve my skills in Excel, data analysis, dashboard creation, and business analytics. Through this analysis, I gained experience in transforming raw data into interactive visual reports and generating insights that support data-driven decisions.
